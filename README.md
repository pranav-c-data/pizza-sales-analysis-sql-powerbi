# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard — SQL & Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed to analyze pizza sales performance across revenue, orders, pizza categories, sizes, time periods, and individual pizza products.

**SQL** was used to query and analyze the sales data, while **Microsoft Power BI** was used to create an interactive dashboard for visualizing KPIs, trends, product performance, and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to help a pizza business understand its **sales performance, customer ordering patterns, and product-level performance**.

The analysis focuses on identifying:

- Overall revenue and order performance
- Peak sales days and months
- Popular pizza categories and sizes
- Best-performing pizza products
- Underperforming pizza products
- Opportunities to improve product and sales strategies

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated from pizza sales?
2. How many orders and pizzas were sold?
3. What is the average order value?
4. How many pizzas are purchased per order on average?
5. Which days of the week generate the most orders?
6. Which months have the highest and lowest order volumes?
7. Which pizza categories contribute the most to sales?
8. Which pizza sizes are most popular?
9. Which pizzas generate the highest revenue?
10. Which pizzas have the highest sales quantity?
11. Which pizzas are included in the highest number of orders?
12. Which pizzas are the weakest performers by revenue, quantity, and orders?
13. Which products may require promotional or menu-strategy changes?

## 💡 Key Insights

### 📅 Ordering Patterns

- Orders are highest toward the end of the working week.
- **Friday** records the highest daily order volume.
- **July** records the highest monthly order volume.
- Weekend demand is also relatively strong.

### 🍕 Category Performance

- **Classic** pizzas contribute the largest share of sales by category.
- **Large** pizzas account for the largest share of sales by pizza size.

### 🏆 Best-Performing Products

- **Thai Chicken Pizza** is the leading pizza by revenue.
- **Classic Deluxe Pizza** is the strongest performer by quantity.
- Several pizzas show similarly high order volumes among the top-performing products.

### 📉 Underperforming Products

- **Brie Carre Pizza** is the weakest performer by revenue and quantity.
- Other lower-performing products include Mediterranean, Spinach Supreme, Green Garden, and Spicy Hawaiian pizzas.

### 💼 Business Implications

The analysis can help management:

- Promote high-performing pizzas during peak periods.
- Review pricing, ingredients, and positioning of underperforming products.
- Optimize inventory based on demand patterns.
- Improve staffing during high-order periods.
- Make data-driven decisions regarding the pizza menu.
